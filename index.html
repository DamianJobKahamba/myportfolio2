Biography

I am Damian Job Kahamba, a dedicated public health professional with over five years of experience leading laboratory and community health initiatives. I improved laboratory audit scores by 20% and increased HIV sample throughput by 40%. Skilled in biostatistics and data analysis using SAS and Python, and in quality management systems, I bring strong leadership and technical expertise to public health programs — committed to driving impactful health outcomes and operational excellence.

My Story

Nov 2019

Career start

Completed my Bachelor's degree in Medical Laboratory Science at Kilimanjaro Christian Medical University College.

Nov 2020

Trials and tribulations

Completed my paid internship at Bugando Medical Center, Central Pathology Laboratory. I had a very hard time finding a job afterward — hard enough that I questioned my career choice.

Apr 2021

Just a chance

Secured my first professional responsibility as a volunteer teaching assistant, responsible for NTA levels 4, 5, and 6 at Muhimbili College of Health and Allied Sciences, Laboratory Department.

Jun 2021

Light at the end of the tunnel

First contract job — hired as Head of the Medical Laboratory Department by Paradigms Institute, Dar es Salaam, overseeing academic quality for laboratory NTA level 4, 5, and 6 students.

Sep 2021

The breakthrough

Hired by the Ministry of Health as a Health Laboratory Scientist and placed at the National Public Health Laboratory, working directly on Tanzania's COVID-19 response.

Apr 2022

Graceful days

Appointed by National Public Health Laboratory management to lead the HIV Molecular Testing department.

Aug 2024

Living the dream

Enrolled in the Master of Public Health program and secured a Graduate Assistant scholarship at Florida Agricultural and Mechanical University.
